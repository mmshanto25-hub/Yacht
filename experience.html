The Rhythm of the Ocean

A Day in the Life Onboard

Every morning begins with uninterrupted horizons; every evening concludes beneath an infinity of stars.

08:00 AM

Sunrise Aft-Deck Breakfast

Freshly brewed espresso and pastries made that morning, fresh tropical fruits, and chilled mimosas as the anchor gently rises.

11:00 AM

Lagoon Exploration & Water Toys

Drop anchor in a crystalline bay. Crew deploys Seabobs, e-foils, paddleboards, and tender trips to untouched private sandbars.

02:00 PM

Flybridge Champagne Luncheon

Grilled Mediterranean branzino and chilled Rosé served alfresco under the shade canopy with panoramic coastal vistas.

06:30 PM

Golden Hour Sunset Cruise

Cocktails on the foredeck sun pads as your captain maneuvers along dramatic sea cliffs while the sky transforms to gold and violet.

09:00 PM

Starlit Dinner & Deck Cinema

Multi-course tasting dinner by your private chef followed by your favorite film projected onto the sundeck under the open stars.

Adrenaline & Discovery

Superyacht Water Toys & Tenders

Every vessel in our fleet comes equipped with state-of-the-art marine toys and custom chase tenders for infinite ocean play.

Seabob F5S Underwater Scooters

Effortlessly glide above coral reefs or dive up to 40 meters beneath the surface with zero emissions and whisper-quiet electric thrust.

Fliteboard e-Foils

Experience the sensation of flying above the water. Electric hydrofoils with handheld wireless Bluetooth remotes suitable for all skill levels.

Williams High-Speed Jet Tenders

Custom-built tenders delivering 45-knot speeds for wakeboarding, water skiing, and dry, elegant transfers directly into harbor quays.

PADI Certified Scuba Gear

Complete onboard compressor systems and dive equipment. Selected yachts include certified onboard PADI dive instructors.

Inflatable Sea Pools & Slides

Jellyfish-proof floating ocean pools with netted swimming perimeters, plus two-story inflatable slides launching from the bridge deck.

Stand-Up Paddleboards & Kayaks

Crystal-clear transparent kayaks and carbon-fiber paddleboards for silent morning explorations of hidden sea caves and estuaries.
