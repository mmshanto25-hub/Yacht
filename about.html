Our heritage

Crafted for Discerning Voyagers

For more than thirty-eight years, Aventis has occupied a revered position in private international yachting. What began as a boutique brokerage for classic New England sailing vessels has evolved into a global superyacht authority with offices in Newport, Monaco, Miami, and London.

We do not merely broker vessels—we curate private oceanic realms. Every captain is personally vetted; every galley is evaluated by Michelin-trained standards; every safety cert is certified to the highest international maritime benchmarks.

How to Charter With Aventis

From your initial inquiry to the moment you step onto the teak deck, our private brokers handle every logistical detail.

01
