French Riviera & Monaco Odyssey

Experience the ultimate Mediterranean week onboard your private superyacht. Hand-curated by our senior captains to avoid crowded ports and maximize tranquil anchorage hours.

145 NMTotal Distance
7 DaysRecommended
May – OctPeak Season
KISMET / LANASuggested Yacht
  1. Day 01

    Monaco Embarkation

    Board in Port Hercules. Welcome champagne, safety briefing, and dinner overlooking the Monte Carlo casino lights.

  2. Day 02

    Cap d'Antibes & Bay of Billionaires

    Morning passage to Cap d'Antibes. Anchor in emerald waters for Seabob exploration and lunch at Hotel du Cap-Eden-Roc.

  3. Day 03

    Cannes & Îles de Lérins

    Cruising between Sainte-Marguerite and Saint-Honorat islands. Visit the ancient monastery vineyard and dine on fresh sea urchin.

  4. Day 04

    Saint-Tropez & Pampelonne Beach

    Tender transfer to Club 55 for celebratory beachside lunch, followed by boutique shopping in historic Saint-Tropez.

  5. Day 05

    Porquerolles Nature Sanctuary

    Drop anchor in untouched national park bays. Sunrise paddleboarding and evening open-air cinema on the yacht's sundeck.

  6. Day 06

    Villefranche-sur-Mer & Eze

    Deep water anchorage beneath the cliffs of medieval Eze. Chef's 7-course farewell tasting dinner with paired Grand Cru wines.

  7. Day 07

    Monaco Disembarkation

    A leisurely morning cruise past the Monaco cliffs. Farewell breakfast on the aft deck and private helicopter transfer to Nice Airport.

The Exumas Sandbar Expedition

365 cays surrounded by the clearest water on Earth. Drift across shallow sandbars unreachable by commercial ships, encounter friendly nurse sharks, and swim in Thunderball Grotto.

110 NMTotal Distance
7 DaysRecommended
Nov – MayPeak Season
M/Y ONYX / Sunseeker 88Suggested Yacht
  1. Day 01

    Nassau to Highbourne Cay

    Embark at Albany Marina. Cruise 35 NM south to Highbourne Cay. Sunset cocktails with nurse sharks swimming right at the marina docks.

  2. Day 02

    Norman's Cay & Sunken Plane

    Snorkel the famous WWII DC-3 plane wreck in 10 feet of turquoise water. Afternoon jet-skiing across empty white sand flats.

  3. Day 03

    Shroud Cay Mangrove River

    Tender through the winding mangrove waterways into the open Exuma Sound. Private beach setup with champagne umbrellas.

  4. Day 04

    Staniel Cay & Thunderball Grotto

    Swim through the underwater cave featured in James Bond's Thunderball. Visit Big Major Cay to swim with the famous Exuma pigs.

  5. Day 05

    Compass Cay Nurse Sharks

    Wade into the calm waters of Compass Cay marina with friendly nurse sharks. Fresh conch salad prepared by your chef on the aft deck.

  6. Day 06

    Warderick Wells Land & Sea Park

    Mooring in the protected Exuma Cays Land & Sea Park. Hike Boo Boo Hill for panoramic views of the sandbar tides.

  7. Day 07

    Return to Nassau / Paradise Island

    Morning passage back to Nassau. Gourmet brunch served under way and limousine transfer to the private jet terminal.
