Charter Transparency

Understanding Charter Terms

We believe in absolute financial clarity. Here is how luxury superyacht charter pricing is structured globally.

1. Base Charter Rate

Covers the hire of the yacht, all licensed maritime crew, insurance for marine risks, and standard vessel amenities for the charter period.

2. APA (Advance Provisioning Allowance)

Typically 30% to 35% of the base charter fee. Held by the captain to cover fuel, guest provisions, fine wines, dockage fees, and communications. Any unused funds are fully refunded at checkout.

3. VAT & Gratuity

Local VAT depends on cruising waters (e.g. France, Italy, Greece). Crew gratuity is customary at 10%–15% of the base rate, given at your discretion for exceptional service.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about planning your private voyage with Aventis.

How far in advance should I reserve a yacht?

For peak Mediterranean summer (July–August) or Caribbean holiday season (Christmas–New Year), we strongly advise booking 6 to 9 months in advance to secure top-tier vessels and prime berths.

Can I customize the daily cruising itinerary?

Absolutely. Your itinerary is entirely bespoke. Your captain evaluates wind and sea conditions each morning to navigate you into the calmest anchorages and secluded bays.

How does food and wine selection work?

Four weeks prior to embarking, you complete our confidential Preference Sheet specifying dietary requests, favorite vintages, and culinary preferences for your private Michelin-trained chef.

Are children and families welcome onboard?

Yes. Our crew are exceptionally family-friendly, organizing water sports, treasure hunts, inflatable slides, and child-safe tender adventures.
