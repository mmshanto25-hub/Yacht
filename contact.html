Brokerage Protocols

Charter Consultation Process

What to expect when engaging with our private brokerage team.

What happens after I submit this inquiry?

A Senior Charter Director evaluates current vessel locations, owner schedules, and seasonal regulations. Within two hours, you receive a curated proposal with verified pricing and captain profiles.

Do you accommodate non-disclosure agreements (NDAs)?

Yes. Discretion is paramount. We routinely execute bilateral NDAs prior to discussing guest identities, security requirements, and confidential rendezvous points.

Can Aventis coordinate aviation and marina security?

Our private concierge arranges private jet charters to Nice, Nassau, or Providence, helicopter transfers directly to the yacht's helipad, and private quay security details.

What contract framework is used?

All charters are executed under the Mediterranean Yacht Brokers Association (MYBA) or International Yacht Brokers Association (IYBA) standard worldwide agreements.
